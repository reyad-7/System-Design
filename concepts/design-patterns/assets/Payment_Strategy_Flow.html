    3. Where the switch went

    The if did not disappear. It got smaller and moved.

    Something still has to turn "this is a CardDto" into "use CardStrategy". That is the switch in the factory. Each branch is now one line that builds an object, instead of 10 to 30 lines of business rules. Once the strategy exists, the controller calls one method and no further if is needed.
